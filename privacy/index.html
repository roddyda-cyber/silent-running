Silent Running Poster: Privacy Policy

Last updated October 30, 2026

Silent Running Poster is a private tool used by one person, the owner of the Facebook Page "My Silent Running" (facebook.com/mysilentrunning). It uploads and schedules that person's own travel videos to their own Page. It is not offered to the public, and nobody else can sign in to it.

What it collects

Nothing from visitors, followers or other Facebook users. The tool only uses an access key for the owner's own Page, which is stored on the owner's personal computer. It does not read messages, comments, followers or anyone's personal information.

What it shares

Nothing. No data is sold, shared or sent anywhere except to Facebook, to publish the owner's own videos.

Data deletion

The tool keeps no data about any user. The owner can stop it at any time by removing the app in Facebook Settings, under Business Integrations, which ends its access. If you believe the tool holds data about you, message the Page at facebook.com/mysilentrunning and it will be confirmed or deleted within 30 days.
